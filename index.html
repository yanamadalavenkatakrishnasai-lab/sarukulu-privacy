Sarukulu (సరుకులు) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Sarukulu is a shopping list, expense and EMI tracking app for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information.

In short

Sarukulu does not collect, store or sell your personal data on any server. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your shopping lists, prices, shop names, bills you scan, expenses, EMIs, customer orders (in Merchant mode) and settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see them. If you uninstall the app or use "Delete everything" in Settings, this data is removed.

The app has no internet access

The app does not ask for network permission and has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Features that use other services

  • Scanning a bill: The photo is read on your phone using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. The photo is not uploaded by the app.
  • Voice entry: When you tap the microphone, your phone's own speech recognition (normally Google's) listens to you. That audio is handled by that service under its own privacy policy, not by Sarukulu. We never receive or keep the recording.
  • Sharing on WhatsApp: When you choose to send a list or a priced order, the app opens WhatsApp with the text ready. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and WhatsApp's own policy then applies. In Merchant mode, the customer's name and phone number you type are kept only on your phone and used only to open that WhatsApp chat.
  • Reminders: EMI reminders are notifications created on your phone. No data leaves the device.

Permissions

  • Notifications: only for your EMI and urgent-item reminders.
  • Biometric / screen lock: only if you turn on App lock, to unlock the app. Your fingerprint or face data is handled by Android and never seen by the app.
  • The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permission. To photograph a bill it opens your phone's own camera app, and voice entry uses your phone's own speech service.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
